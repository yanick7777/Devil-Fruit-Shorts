## 1. Fruit design sheet

Source: the main One Piece Wiki, read through search results (the wiki host is blocked here): **"a blue pear, albeit with a 'cracked' lower appearance."**
Other fan wikis disagree (grey pear, blue pineapple), and an official design was published in October 2025 that I couldn't view from here.
**This design is unconfirmed: if you have a reference image of the official fruit, send it and I'll match it.**
