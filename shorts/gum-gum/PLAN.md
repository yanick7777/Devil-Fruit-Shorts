## 1. Fruit design sheet

Source: One Piece Wiki, *Gomu Gomu no Mi*, Appearance (read through search results; the wiki host is blocked here):

> "The Gomu Gomu no Mi is a violet, spherical shaped fruit, similar to a cantaloupe, with a stem sprouting out of the top,
> with swirls that go in a curly 'S' like-pattern. Its overall appearance greatly resembles a Yubari King."

## 3. Cast (all full-body)

- **Host:** same as before (black messy hair, round glasses, teal hoodie, mug), plus **Gear 2** (pink skin, steam), **Gear 4** (puffed up, black Haki fists, bouncing) and **Gear 5** (white hair and clothes, cloud collar) versions.
- **Enemy (new, original):** bald gunslinger with dark shades, goatee, green vest over a grey tee, boots and a pistol. Not the bandana pirate from the Mera Mera short.
- **Little Luffy:** straw hat, scar under the left eye, red tee, blue shorts, sandals.
- **Nika:** shown only as a glowing white sun-god silhouette with drum-beat rings.
