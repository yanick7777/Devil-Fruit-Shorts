## 3. Cast (all full-body)

- **Host:** same as before, plus a surgical mask and scalpel in the surgeon shot.
- **Enemy (new, original):** pirate chef with a tall chef hat, curled moustache, red neck scarf and white apron.
- **Corazon:** very tall; black hood with blond hair framing his face, black triangle eye makeup, red painted clown smile, cigarette, black feather mantle and long black coat, white shirt with red hearts, striped pants.

Sets (new to this short): soft pink hearts, yellow rays, heart monitor, operating room, clinic hall stage, pine forest, ship's galley, giant chessboard, city park, lab, x-ray lightbox, clock room, dusk hill, snowy northern town, party room (`review/bg_sheet.png`). Shared with the template only: desk room and outro purple rays.

New effects: the fruit pulsing like a heartbeat, the blue Room dome spreading from the host's hand, the chef sliced into three floating pieces that stay alive, Shambles swaps with blue rings at both ends (chess pieces, then people), a beating heart in a cube pulled out of the chef's chest, Gamma Knife's glowing blade and the chef's skeleton lighting up from the inside, a giant hourglass for the twist, the host fading as he gives away eternal youth, little Law's patches fading in sparkles, a musical-chairs Shambles. Kept from the template: evolve cross-dissolve with flash and glow ring, gold FORM banners, halos, shine lines, impact stars with 7-frame shakes, !? and ? marks, corner overlays (scope, ROOM meter, magnifier).

Sound: heartbeat, Room hum, slash, zip swaps, zap, clock ticks, fade, shimmer, level-up. No music.
