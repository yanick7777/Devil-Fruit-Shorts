# Voiceover scripts

Read each script in one take, top to bottom, with a short pause (about half a second) between lines.
Send the audio file and I'll split it at the pauses, match each piece to its line, and re-time every shot to your voice.
Pronunciation notes: *Paramecia* = pa-ra-MEE-see-ya, *Haki* = HAH-kee, *Nika* = NEE-ka, *Corazon* = ko-ra-ZON.

## Gum-Gum Fruit (Gomu Gomu no Mi)

```
You take a bite of a weird purple fruit.
Don't panic, that's just a Devil Fruit.
Out of every fruit in the sea,
you got the Gum-Gum Fruit,
a Paramecia that turns your body into rubber and unlocks a whole list of gears.
First, Gear Two pumps your blood so fast you steam and move in a blur.
Then Gear Three blows air into your bones for a fist the size of a house.
Gear Four fills your muscles with air and Haki, so you bounce around like a giant rubber ball.
Your strongest form is Gear Five: your hair turns white and you bend the world like a cartoon.
But Gum-Gum was never its real name.
It's secretly the Human-Human Fruit, Model: Nika, and the government renamed it to hide it.
Shanks's crew stole it from a government ship, and little Luffy ate it by accident.
Users of this fruit never need a ladder.
Which fruit do you want next? Follow for more.
```

164 words, about 46 seconds at a fast, excited pace.

## String-String Fruit (Ito Ito no Mi)

```
You take a bite of a weird lilac fruit.
Don't panic, that's just a Devil Fruit.
Out of every fruit in the sea,
you got the String-String Fruit,
a Paramecia that shoots strings sharp enough to slice stone, and comes with a whole set of tricks.
First, you hook your strings onto the clouds and walk right across the sky.
Then you tie invisible strings onto people and control them like puppets.
You can even make a clone of yourself out of string that can't feel any pain.
And once you awaken it, the ground and buildings around you turn into strings too.
But its scariest trick is called the Birdcage.
A cage of strings drops over a whole island and slowly closes in, slicing everything inside.
Doflamingo got it from Trebol, and he even stitches his own wounds shut with it.
Users of this fruit never lose at cat's cradle.
Which fruit do you want next? Follow for more.
```

160 words. Pronunciation: Paramecia = pa-ra-MEE-see-ya, Doflamingo = doe-fla-MING-go, Trebol = TREH-bol.
